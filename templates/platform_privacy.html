Exportly Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 (Enterprise Ready)

At Exportly ("we," "our," or "us"), accessible from our platform, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors and users. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Exportly and how we use it. This policy is designed to comply with global standards including GDPR, CCPA, and the India DPDP Act.

1. Definitions & Roles

  • Data Controller: Exportly is the controller of your account data (name, email, billing info).
  • Data Processor: For the content you upload to your export website (products, text, images), YOU (the Exporter) are the Data Controller, and Exportly acts as a Data Processor on your behalf.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, email address, company name, phone number, and encrypted password.
  • Business Data: Products, images, pages, and text you add to your Exportly website.
  • Usage & Technical Data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent (collected via cookies).
  • Payment Information: Processed securely by third-party gateways (Razorpay/Stripe). We do not store full card details.

3. Administrator Access & Platform Monitoring

To maintain platform security, prevent fraud, provide customer support, and ensure compliance with our Terms of Service, authorized Exportly administrators may access your account information and website content. This access is strictly limited to authorized personnel and is governed by strict confidentiality obligations.

4. Third-Party Sub-Processors & AI

We use third-party services to operate our platform. By using Exportly, you consent to the use of these sub-processors:

  • AI Features: When you use AI descriptions, text is sent to Groq API. This data is processed securely and is not used to train public AI models.
  • Cloud Infrastructure: Hosting (Render/VPS), Database (Neon/PostgreSQL), and Image Storage (Cloudinary).
  • Communication: Emails are sent via SMTP providers.

5. Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard security measures (including SSL encryption, secure servers, and strict access controls) to protect your data. We retain your personal information only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this policy. If you delete your account, we will purge your personal data within 30 days, except where legally required to retain it.

6. International Data Transfers

Your information, including Personal Data, may be transferred to — and maintained on — computers located outside of your state, province, country, or other governmental jurisdiction where the data protection laws may differ. We ensure appropriate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) are in place for such transfers.

7. Your Data Protection Rights (GDPR, CCPA, DPDP)

Depending on your location, you have the following rights:

  • The right to access, rectify, or erase your data.
  • The right to data portability.
  • The right to withdraw consent at any time.
  • Enterprise Clients: We offer a comprehensive Data Processing Agreement (DPA) upon request for enterprise compliance.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
